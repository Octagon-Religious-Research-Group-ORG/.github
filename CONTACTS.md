# Contacts

How to reach the Octagon Religious-Research Group (ORG). Every address and channel here is one ORG
publishes for this purpose; nobody's personal details are listed. The same list opens as one window
from **Reach out** on every ORG page on GitHub.

*Last checked 2026-10-13.*
