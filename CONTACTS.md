# Contacts

How to reach the Octagon Religious-Research Group (ORG). Every address and channel here is one ORG
publishes for this purpose; nobody's personal details are listed. The same list opens as one window
from **Reach out** on every ORG page on GitHub.

*Last checked 2026-10-22.*

## Telegram, where ORG gathers

Tripp OK'd every link below for these pages. Two chats are open to anyone; two groups are private.

| Where | Who it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [ORG (Public)](https://t.me/ORG_Spirituality), @ORG_Spirituality | ORG's public chat, open to anyone. In its own words, "an unrestricted public chat": what members say there doesn't always reflect ORG's views |
| [ORG Events and Ceremonies](https://t.me/+ccL8NUffhuU0ZGJh) | A collaboration between ORG and Entheo Community. orgspirituality.org's [Community page](https://orgspirituality.org/community) sends people here for news of the monthly meditation ceremony |
| [ORG (Voting)](https://t.me/+1hlK0bCSBzwyOTkx) 🔒 | Private: for confirmed ORG members, to vote on all of ORG's decisions |
| [ORG EaMs (Entheo-approved Members)](https://t.me/+9gpCph_VVqUwN2Zh) 🔒 | Private: for ORG members who have completed an approved form of entheogen safety training |
| [@Tripp_ORG](https://t.me/Tripp_ORG) | Tripp, ORG's founder, as the Contact Us section of [orgspirituality.org/about](https://orgspirituality.org/about#contact) lists him (he's also @Tripp_ORG.99 on Signal). The Community page asks people who'd like to join to message him with their preferred name |

**The private groups admit people by hand.** Tripp approves each request once that person's
[membership form](https://www.icloud.com/pages/051s7hMIDB_LNvP2CEYUZfkew#Membership) (or, for EaMs,
their EaM form) is complete, so it's fine to ask to join now: the request simply waits until then.
